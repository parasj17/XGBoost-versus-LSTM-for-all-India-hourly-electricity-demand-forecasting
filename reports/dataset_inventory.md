## 2. Primary electricity dataset (Mendeley Data)

- Title: *Electricity Demand, Solar and Wind Generation Data (September 2021–June 2025) of India at 1-hour interval*, Version 2, DOI 10.17632/y58jknpgs8.2, published 2025-09-15, CC BY 4.0. Authors per the landing page: D. Mukherjee, K. Kalita, S. Kumar (IIT Guwahati; Power Grid Corporation of India).
- Landing page text (verified by fetch): the description says it comes from NERLDC, Grid-India, covers "India's five combined regional grids" at 1-hour intervals, and gives the period as **Sep 2021 – Dec 2023**, whereas the **title says Sep 2021 – June 2025**. The page lists no column definitions, units or time zone.
- **Actual workbook contents (verified):** 2021-09-01 00:00 → 2025-06-24 23:00. The data do **not** reach 30 June 2025, and they are **not hourly** for most of the period (see `dataset_audit.md`).

## 3. Weather source — Open-Meteo Historical Weather API (verified)

- Endpoint `https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive`; ERA5 (0.25°, hourly) available 1940–present; ERA5-Land 0.1°.
- Hourly variables confirmed from the docs: `temperature_2m`, `relative_humidity_2m`, `dew_point_2m`, `precipitation`, `wind_speed_10m`, `cloud_cover`, `shortwave_radiation` (plus others).
- `timezone` parameter accepts `Asia/Kolkata`.
- A 3-day pilot for 12 cities (no data saved) returned 72 hourly rows each, 0 nulls, IST timestamps. **Full download done 2026-10-13** (owner-approved city list): 12 CSVs in `data/raw/weather/`, each 33,432 rows (2021-09-01 00:00 → 2025-06-24 23:00 IST), 0 nulls, time grid identical to the demand grid; value ranges plausible (e.g. Delhi 3.7–46.5 °C). **Wind speed is in km/h** (API default); precipitation = preceding-hour sum (mm); `shortwave_radiation` = preceding-hour mean (W/m²).
- Free tier is for non-commercial use; attribution (Zippenfenig 2023; Copernicus/ERA5) required. Rate limits not published in the page I read; the script throttles to 1 request/second.

## 4. Calendar / holidays

- `india.gov.in/calendar` returned **HTTP 403** to my fetch tool; **not verified**. It is a general calendar page and I could not confirm it offers downloadable historic lists.
- Used instead: DoPT (Government of India) Office Memoranda on central-government holidays for 2021–2025, reproduced on staffnews.in. These are secondary reproductions of the official memoranda; spot-check against dopt.gov.in PDFs (one 2022 entry is flagged in the CSV).
- Only the central (Delhi) gazetted list was obtained. **State/regional holidays are not collected.**

## 6. Zenodo 14983362 — verified details (downloaded files only)

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| `corrected_daily_energy_met_MU.csv` | Columns: `Date`, 36 states/UTs + `AMNSIL`, `Damodar Valley Corporation`, `Total` (39 columns). Unit **MU** (million units = GWh of energy per day), **not MW**. Range **2013-01-01 → 2024-04-28** (not through Dec 2024). 3,955 rows, of which **55 duplicated dates** (10 with differing `Total`, up to 333 MU apart) and **236 missing days** (largest gap 82 days from 2013-01-08). Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep are 100 % empty; Dadra/Daman 72 % empty after 2021-09 (merged UTs). `Total` is never null; states sum to `Total` within 1 % on 93.5 % of days. |
| Per-state weather CSV (e.g. `NCT_of_Delhi.csv`) | Daily min/mean/max of ERA5-type variables: 10 m u/v wind, 2 m temperature (K), 2 m dew point (K), surface solar radiation downwards, total cloud cover, UTCI. 1979-01-01 → 2024-04-27 (16,554 rows). Daily only. Delhi daily mean temperature validated against our Open-Meteo ERA5 download on 969 overlapping days: correlation 0.999, mean difference -0.19 C, MAE 0.31 C (0.22 C on a UTC day), so it is ERA5-derived as assumed. The two first UTCI rows have identical min/mean values, a possible data artefact. |
| `weighted_daily_mean_utci_india.csv` | `date`, `utci` (Kelvin), 1979-01-01 → **2020-12-31** only — **does not overlap** the Mendeley period. Unusable for this project as is; the per-state files (to 2024-04) must be re-aggregated instead. |
| Cross-check with Mendeley | On 955 overlapping days (2021-09-01 → 2024-04-28, days with 24 valid hours), Mendeley daily mean MW × 24 / 1000 vs Zenodo `Total` (MU): correlation 0.997, mean ratio 1.001, MAPE 0.57 %. Strong independent support that Mendeley `NLDC_DEMAND` is all-India *demand met*. |
| Corrected vs uncorrected | `daily_energy_met_MU.csv` and `corrected_*` have identical shape/columns/dates; `Total` differs on only 2 of 3,900 unique days (largest 2,055 MU), so the correction fixes a few gross errors. Use the `corrected_*` files. |
| Peak files | `peak_met_MW`, `corrected_peak_met_MW`, `peak_unmet_MW`: same layout and dates; national `Total` peak-met maximum **249,623 MW** (consistent with Mendeley's 248,688 MW hourly maximum in 2024), peak unmet maximum 12,251 MW. |
| Per-state weather files | 35 files, each 16,554 rows (1979-01-01 → 2024-04-27) × 22 columns, 291 null cells per file in the four I checked. `Dadra_and_Nagar_Haveli.csv` and `Daman_and_Diu.csv` are byte-identical (same grid cell/merged UT). Lakshadweep and Andaman files exist although their energy columns are empty. |
