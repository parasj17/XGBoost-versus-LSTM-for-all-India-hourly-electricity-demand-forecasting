# Data availability notes: solar/wind forecasts (plan task 1.9) and EV data (task 9.6)

Both items were investigated with a general web search on 2026-10-16 (search summaries only; the original documents were not opened, so every statement below should be checked at its source before it is used in a publication).

## 1.9 Are historical solar and wind generation forecasts available for India?
**Finding: no public archive of historical forecasts was found.**
- The Mendeley dataset used as the demand source contains *actual* solar and wind generation, not forecasts: [Electricity Demand, Solar and Wind Generation Data (September 2021 - June 2025) of India](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/y58jknpgs8).
- Grid-India publishes daily power-supply-position reports (PDF, actual values; the same report notes limited visibility of embedded solar), for example [NLDC PSP report of 7 June 2023](https://report.grid-india.in/ReportData/Daily%20Report/PSP%20Report/2023-2024/June%202023/07.06.23_NLDC_PSP.pdf).
- Forecasting and scheduling of renewable generation does exist operationally: renewable energy management centres forecast on very-short-term, intra-day, day-ahead and week-ahead bases ([ADB / Asia Clean Energy Forum overview](https://asiacleanenergyforum.adb.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Renewable-Energy-Management-Centers-in-India.pdf), [POSOCO solar forecasting presentation](https://posoco.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Solar_Power_Forecasting_Session_3_Day_2_compressed.pdf)); generators face forecasting-and-scheduling and deviation-settlement rules ([history of the 2013 requirement](https://www.eco-business.com/ms/news/india-tells-wind-farms-forecast-power-or-face-fines/), [Renewable Watch, 2025](https://renewablewatch.in/2025/04/09/improving-accuracy-fs-regulations-call-for-a-relook-to-ensure-developer-compliance/)). I found no evidence that these forecasts or schedules are published as a historical dataset.
- Consequence for this project: the frozen models use only *past actual* solar and wind (up to the forecast origin). Using forecast generation would require requesting schedule/forecast data from Grid-India or CERC; weather forecasts could be used as a proxy but the archive checked here (Open-Meteo previous runs) has only temperature for 2021-2023.
- Status of task 1.9: investigated; not available publicly; no further work planned.

## 9.6 EV features
**Finding: no usable historical EV load data, and the effect is far below the forecast error.**
- The electricity needed to charge the existing EV stock was about 0.2% of India's final electricity consumption in 2024, projected to reach about 1.1% by 2030 (IEA figures quoted by [Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/from-fossil-to-flexible-advancing-indias-road-transport-electrification/clean-electricity-supply-must-keep-pace)); IESA expects about 3% of grid demand by 2031-32 ([Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/iesa-report-predicts-operational-evs-in-india-to-cross-28-million-units-by-2030)). The test MAPE of the models is 0.5-2.7%, i.e. of the same order as the whole EV load in the test period.
- CEA reports monthly electricity consumption at public charging stations (about 763 MU for April 2024 to February 2025, concentrated in Delhi and Maharashtra, [The Week, citing CEA](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/05/15/des70-dl-ev-charging-report.html)); the public open datasets found are monthly utility totals ([Telangana EV charging stations consumption dataset](https://aikosh.indiaai.gov.in/home/datasets/details/telangana_ev_charging_stations_electricity_consumption_dataset.html)) or synthetic sessions. No open hourly load profile for Indian EV charging was found.
- Decision: task 9.6 is closed as not feasible with reliable data and not material for this forecast accuracy. It remains a possible extension if hourly charging data becomes available.
